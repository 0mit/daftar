Machinery: daftar's mechanisms, drawn

daftar's own mechanisms are recorded here as a garden records anything: as beans and mappings in a demo garden, each with its source. They are drawn by factory, the application layer that draws a garden's mechanisms at four levels of detail. This site publishes what it drew: the report below, one offline file.

Five mechanisms, and where they meet

A writer's change passes the gate before it enters the garden's history, and the journal gives each change its heading from the clock, in the same commit. Another clone of the garden, or another session's branch, reaches the same history through the merge. Another garden meets this one only through the mycelium, by proposal. Agreements record what moved, and the ledger reads what is owed from it.

Four levels, four questions

Each mechanism is drawn at four levels of detail, from what it is to what exactly runs. The levels and their questions are the drawing tool's own definitions, recorded in the demo garden:

lensdrawn aswhat it shows
orientstorywhat the drawn thing does and what it is built from, for someone who has never touched it: its purpose, three to five stages in the order work flows, its outcome, and each technology's own documentation. No address, port or live value
understandschematichow the parts co-operate, where the work is decided and where it is fragile: parts by what they do, flows as verbs, decisions as gates, boundaries where ownership or network changes. No address or live value
operatehealth-chainwhat the person on call must know first, now: the drawn thing's own vital sign in a shape native to it (a row of `view_archetypes`), the evidence beneath it, what the page cannot see, and where a person may act
inspectanatomywhat exactly the parts are and how that is known: a card of each part's own facts with their provenance, the steps, the wiring, and what is still open

The report

One tab per mechanism, and one for the beans they involve. The level switch moves between the four levels; a dotted term, or a part of a drawing, carries a definition — hover over it, or tap it.

Open the report on its own page. It is the drawing tool's own document and runs its own script; the rest of this site runs none. Its drawings are made for a wide screen: on a phone, turn it sideways. The badge beside its title is the drawing tool's reading of the demo garden's own history, which carries no release tag — it says "unversioned"; the language it was drawn with is named below.

What this demo cannot show

  • Operate reads live data. The demo garden has no probe, no metric and no monitoring behind it, so every part reads “no signal”, with the tool's own reason. That is the demo garden's true state, not a fault. No mechanism declares a live shape, because declaring one without data would be a false claim. For each, the shape it would take:
  • The gate: it would take the funnel shape with live data. A funnel counts the commits that arrive, those stopped at each check and those recorded; this garden exports no count of its hook's verdicts, and no monitor reads one.
  • The journal: it would take the roster shape with live data. A roster shows who writes, and how much, from a live series; nothing here measures the journal.
  • The merge: no shape of the view asset fits it. A merge waiting for a person is a fact of the ledger, merge_open on the bean, which the gate reads; it is not a metric.
  • The mycelium: no shape of the view asset fits it. What waits to be taken is a proposal file beside the gardens, not a metric.
  • The ledger: no shape of the view asset fits it. What falls due next is answered on demand by bin/dmstale.py and bin/dmledger.py, not by a metric.
  • Actions. None is placed: an action asks for a tool that only a host's own configuration can run.
  • Author. The report's editor for the drawing's record works on a copy in your browser, and nothing reads what it exports.
  • The live page and the dashboards the drawing tool can serve for a running garden are not part of this site.
