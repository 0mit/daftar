A cost shared between two people

Sam and Ali bought a camera together. Ali paid for it; Sam uses it more, so they agreed that Sam bears two parts of its cost and Ali one. The notebook records what moved and who bears it. What one owes the other is read from that, and never written down beside it.

garden-sam · daftar v0.34.0 · the day held at 2026-10-27

What the notebook records

An agreement is a contract bean. This one is owned by neither of them: it ends at the crown, where every chain of ownership ends, in its branch logos — the word, for what exists by being said and agreed — and both parties answer for it. A party with accepted said yes on that day. The transaction says what moved: an amount, the day, who paid, and who bears it in whole-number shares.

beans/shared-camera.md---
bean: shared-camera
genos: contract
title: "shared-camera — Sam and Ali bought a camera together"
status: active
summary: "Sam and Ali share a camera; Ali paid for it, and they bear its cost two to one."
nature: lekton
identity:
  status: confirmed
  anchors:
    - { key: contract_id, value: "contract:shared-camera", class: logical, establishing: true }
provenance: { src: asserted-by-human, by: "sam", as_of: 2026-09-17 }
owned_by: { legal: { crown: logos } }
responsibility: { legal: { parties: true } }
parties:
  sam: { who: { bean: sam }, accepted: 2026-09-10 }
  ali: { who: { bean: ali }, accepted: 2026-09-10 }
over:
  - { what: "a camera the two of them use" }
words: { form: spoken, agreed: 2026-09-10 }
transactions:
  camera:
    what: "the camera, bought online"
    amount: { count: "90.00", unit: XTS }
    day: 2026-09-10
    paid_by:
      - { party: ali }
    borne_by:
      - { party: sam, share: 2 }
      - { party: ali, share: 1 }
---
Sam uses the camera more, so Sam bears two parts of its cost and Ali one.

An amount is exact: a count written as a string, with no more places than the currency uses, and never a float. XTS is the code ISO reserves for testing; a garden writes its own currency's.

What the tools say

dmledger reads the transaction. Ali paid 90 XTS and bears one part in three; each bearer owes each payer its share of what that payer paid:

~/garden-sam$ python3 bin/dmledger.py shared-camera
== shared-camera — shared-camera — Sam and Ali bought a camera together
   parties: ali, sam
   transactions (1):
     camera  2026-09-10  90 XTS  paid by ali  · borne by ali 30 XTS, sam 60 XTS  "the camera, bought online"
   XTS  position (paid less borne): ali +60 XTS · sam -60 XTS
   sam owes ali 60 XTS

Had the camera cost 100.00 XTS, a third would not come out even in XTS's two decimal places. In a throwaway clone of the garden, dmsafe changes the one count, and dmledger reads it again:

~$ git clone -q ~/garden-sam ~/garden-sam-scratch
~/garden-sam-scratch$ python3 bin/dmsafe.py flow-set beans/shared-camera.md transactions.camera.amount.count '"100.00"' --expect 1
dmsafe: removed nothing; added nothing
~/garden-sam-scratch$ python3 bin/dmledger.py shared-camera
== shared-camera — shared-camera — Sam and Ali bought a camera together
   parties: ali, sam
   transactions (1):
     camera  2026-09-10  100 XTS  paid by ali  · borne by ali 100/3 XTS, sam 200/3 XTS  "the camera, bought online"
       NOTE 200/3, 100/3 XTS (does not come out even in XTS's 2 places — who takes the remainder is a clause)
   XTS  position (paid less borne): ali +200/3 XTS · sam -200/3 XTS
   sam owes ali 200/3 XTS (does not come out even in XTS's 2 places — who takes the remainder is a clause)

What it shows

  • What is owed is read, never written. There is no balance to store: a stored balance is a second copy of what the transactions say, and it drifts the first time a transaction is added and the balance is not.
  • Nothing is rounded. Every share is a fraction. One that does not come out even is shown as the fraction it is, and who takes the remainder is something the parties agree, in a clause.
  • No currency is privileged. Every amount stays in the currency it was paid in; no factor joins two currencies, and a rate is an observation someone made. No privileged sibling.
  • An agreement between two people may be owned by neither. Then both answer for it, and the two notebooks' records of it agree about its owner. The crown.
