Use cases

Each case is a situation, the beans that record it, and what the tools print about it. They happen in two demo gardens: garden-sam, kept by sam, and garden-ali, kept by ali, who shares costs with him and keeps a notebook of her own.

  • Your machines, and the domain they answer on

    A NAS at home serves a website on a registered domain; a rented server beside it.

    dmcheck · dmcursor · dmstale

  • A cost shared between two people

    Ali paid for a camera; sam bears two parts of it. Who owes whom is read, never written.

    dmledger · dmsafe

  • A loan repaid in instalments

    Six monthly instalments and a clause for one paid late; the first is paid, the next falls due.

    dmledger · dmstale · dmsafe

  • A statement kept, its secret left out

    A bank statement identified by its bytes, and its lines kept as a capture without the card number.

    the gate

  • A dinner where something was agreed

    An evening, the people at it, and the loan agreed there in spoken words.

    dmcal

  • Two people's notebooks meet

    First contact, a proposal made, read and taken, and ali's own yes, written by her.

    dmpropose · dmledger · dmsafe

  • A rehearsal, before it counts

    A garden grown to try the loan's crossing, refused as fact and taken into a copy as a rehearsal.

    germinate · dmpropose

  • An agent of any make, and an assistant with no shell

    The gate as the arbiter between agents, and a chat assistant's proposal taken in by the gardener.

    dmcheck · dmpropose

  • On Windows

    The same tools in PowerShell: which Python, UTF-8, and the three things PowerShell 5.1 does differently.

    INSTALL.md

How these pages are made

  • site/build.py clones daftar at v0.33.1 and grows the two gardens with seed/germinate.py.
  • It commits the recipes of seed/COOKBOOK.md one at a time, and runs the commands shown. What each block shows below a command is what that command printed. A line … marks lines left out; no line is edited.
  • The clock is held at 2026-10-27 09:00 UTC, so a date the tools count to reads the same on every build.
  • A garden's id differs in every build, because germination writes a random seed into the first commit; so does every fingerprint and name that carries one. They are marked with a dotted underline.
  • The directory that holds the gardens is shown as ~. Inputs the build wrote, such as a block handed to dmsafe, are shown as inputs.

Your situation is not here? Describe it in your own words — the form asks for the situation, never for anything from your garden.

Every output on these pages was produced by daftar v0.33.1 on two demo gardens, the clock held at 2026-10-27 09:00 UTC.
