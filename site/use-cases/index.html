Use cases

You do not work in the notebook: you talk to your agents, in your own words, and they keep it. Each case below starts there — what a person says and what they read back — and then shows what happens underneath, for anyone who wants to know. They happen in two demo gardens: garden-sam, kept by Sam, and garden-ali, kept by Ali, who shares costs with him and keeps a notebook of her own.

  • Your machines, and the domain they answer on

    A NAS at home serves a website on a registered domain; a server is rented beside it. You say what you run, and are told in time what is running out.

    dmcursor · dmstale

  • Money between two people

    A camera shared, a loan agreed over dinner and repaid monthly, and the bank statement that proves it. Who owes whom is read, never written.

    dmledger · dmstale · dmcal · dmsafe

  • Two people's notebooks meet

    Neither agent writes in the other's notebook: a proposal crosses, is read and taken, and Ali writes her own yes. And first, a rehearsal that is never taken for fact.

    dmpropose

  • Several agents, one notebook

    A coding agent, an assistant in a chat window, a small model on your own computer: none remembers the others, and the gate judges them all alike.

    dmcheck · dmpropose

Four ways to read a case

Each case can be read by four people, the way the machinery is drawn at four levels — each for someone with a different question.

  • The person (Orient) — what do I say, and what do I get back? The conversation, in plain words.
  • The curious (Understand) — what does the agent do with my words? Each tool it calls, why, and what the tool answered.
  • The keeper (Operate) — is it working? The tools' own output, whole, and what a refusal says.
  • The auditor (Inspect) — what exactly was written? Every file, as it stands.

The conversations are written for these pages. Everything the agents report in them — every figure, date and refusal — is what the tools print, and that output is real.

Starting

Tell your agent to read INSTALL.md and follow it: it grows your notebook, with you as its gardener. On a Windows computer the same holds, with three differences the agent has to know — Starting on Windows.

How these pages are made

  • site/build.py clones daftar at v0.34.0 and grows the two gardens with seed/germinate.py.
  • It commits the recipes of seed/COOKBOOK.md one at a time, and runs the commands shown. What each block shows below a command is what that command printed. A line … marks lines left out; no line is edited.
  • The clock is held at 2026-10-27 09:00 UTC, so a date the tools count to reads the same on every build.
  • A garden's id differs in every build, because germination writes a random seed into the first commit; so does every fingerprint and name that carries one. They are marked with a dotted underline.
  • The directory that holds the gardens is shown as ~. Inputs the build wrote, such as a block handed to dmsafe, are shown as inputs.

Your situation is not here? Describe it in your own words — the form asks for the situation, never for anything from your garden.

Every output on these pages was produced by daftar v0.34.0 on two demo gardens, the clock held at 2026-10-27 09:00 UTC — except the machinery's drawing, its commands and its report, made from a garden grown by daftar v0.33.1.
