A statement kept, its secret left out

Sam paid for Ali's washing machine by card, and the bank's statement shows it. The statement is a document: its identity is its content, so a changed byte is another document. Its lines are the bank's truth, kept as a dated copy, with the card number left out and the reason for leaving it out written down.

garden-sam · daftar v0.34.0 · the day held at 2026-10-27

What the notebook records

The bank wrote the statement, so the bank owns it; Sam holds the copy, and located_at says where it is. content_hash is the SHA-256 of the file's bytes. The lines are a capture: never authoritative, keyed by what would make it stale, and carrying no secret — redactions says what was left out, and why. taken_at is the moment it was taken, in milliseconds.

What the tools say

The cookbook gives the command that prints the hash on each system:

content_hash is sha256: and the file's SHA-256 in lowercase hexadecimal: sha256sum <file> prints it on Linux, shasum -a 256 <file> on macOS, and in PowerShell "sha256:" + (Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 <file>).Hash.ToLower() — Get-FileHash prints capitals, and the gate refuses them.

In a throwaway clone of the garden, the build writes the hash in capitals, as Get-FileHash prints it, in a block for dmsafe:

input: anchor.yamlidentity:
  status: confirmed
  anchors:
    - { key: content_hash, value: "sha256:9F86D081884C7D659A2FEAA0C55AD015A3BF4F1B2B0B822CD15D6C15B0F00A08", class: logical, establishing: true }

dmsafe finds nothing lost — the value changed, and no leaf path went — and the gate refuses the commit:

What it shows

  • A document is identified by its bytes. A copy anywhere with the same hash is the same statement; one changed byte is another.
  • An anchor has one spelling. The gate refuses another spelling rather than guess that two spellings are one value, and its refusal says which spelling it takes.
  • A capture is a copy, knowingly taken. A fact is authoritative here, a pointer to whoever owns it, or a capture: dated, keyed by what would make it stale, never applied back without reading the source again, and never holding a secret.
