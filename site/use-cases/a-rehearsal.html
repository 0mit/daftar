A rehearsal, before it counts

Before the loan crosses to Ali's notebook for real, Sam tries the crossing in a garden grown for the purpose. The rehearsal says it is one, in its own manifest. Ali's notebook marks it as one in its own record, and will not take it in as a fact about the world. A copy of her notebook takes it in as a rehearsal, and every bean it writes says so.

garden-sam, garden-sam-rehearsal and garden-ali · daftar v0.33.1 · the day held at 2026-10-27

What the notebooks record

The rehearsal is grown by germination, never cloned: a clone is the same garden, with the same id, and what it proposes is that garden's word. Its GARDEN.md says what it rehearses:

GARDEN.md---
garden: garden-sam-rehearsal
extends: std-vocab@21.0
daftar_release: "v0.33.1"  # the daftar release this garden runs; bin/dmupgrade.py moves it
gardener: sam                      # who keeps this garden: its first bean
test: "a rehearsal of the washer loan"
…

Ali records the rehearsal garden as she records any garden, and marks it test — her own record, whatever its proposals say:

> Taken in with `--as-test` from a TEST garden (a rehearsal of the washer loan): a rehearsal, not anyone's word about the world — this whole bean is the rehearsal's.

What it shows

  • A rehearsal is grown, never copied. A clone of a real garden carries its id, and what it proposed would be that garden's word.
  • Each gardener marks it in their own records. The rehearsal says so in its manifest; the receiving garden says so on its own garden bean, and holds later proposals to it whatever they say.
  • A rehearsal never passes for a person's word. A garden that is not a test garden takes one in only with --as-test, and then every bean it writes says so.
